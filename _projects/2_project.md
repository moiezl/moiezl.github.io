<!-- The Brief Simplified -->
<h1>The Brief Simplified</h1>
<ul>
    <li>
        <strong>Game Concept:</strong>
        <p>
            Design a horror game where the player starts outside a haunted area and must enter through a mysterious gate.
            The open-world environment spans decrepit houses and misty woods, where players collect clues to uncover the ghost’s identity.
        </p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Gameplay and Experience:</strong>
        <p>
            The game relies on tension-building exploration. Players wield a flickering torch as the primary light source, navigating dimly lit areas that evoke unease. 
            Eerie music swells and subtle whispering noises punctuate key moments, intensifying the feeling of unsafety. The atmosphere is inspired by horror classics like Silent Hill, Resident Evil, Dead by Daylight, and Phasmophobia.
        </p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Development and Tools:</strong>
        <p>
            <em>Engines &amp; Languages:</em> Unity and C#<br>
            <em>Asset Creation:</em> Photoshop for art and animations (with environmental assets sourced under Creative Commons licenses).<br>
            <em>Documentation:</em> Detailed level designs, story outlines, and game mechanics documented using Google Suite.
        </p>
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Core Features Demonstrated:</strong>
        <p>
            The game features intricately designed levels where clues are hidden in atmospheric environments, interactive puzzles (such as a light switch puzzle near a power box),
            and a user interface that tracks progress and difficulty adjustments.
        </p>
    </li>
</ul>

<!-- Mistakes and Improvements -->
<h1>Mistakes</h1>
<p>
    As this project was among my first non-tutorial endeavors, I encountered several challenges:
</p>
<ul>
    <li>
        <strong>Lighting Optimization:</strong> The advanced post-processing effects caused lag and performance issues.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Puzzle Functionality:</strong> Some puzzles, like the light switch challenge, did not register correctly, occasionally focusing on the power box and becoming unclickable at certain resolutions.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Asset Importing:</strong> Difficulties with asset imports led to inconsistent visuals during development.
    </li>
</ul>
<p>
    Future improvements include refining the lighting system, conducting more extensive testing to ensure puzzle responsiveness, and optimizing asset management to enhance overall performance.
</p>
